# 201H

201H projects (HRS Chapter 201H) are state-certified affordable housing projects that can be exempted from county zoning and development rules project by project (HRS § 201H-38), in exchange for making at least half the units affordable. It runs in `src/calculations/calc-h201.ts`, program id `h201`. Everything not listed here follows [Housing Policy Simulator logic](../simulator-logic.md).

## Envelope

The model does not assume any project-specific 201H exemption: it builds to the underlying zoning.

| Rule | 201H |
| --- | --- |
| FAR | The base zone's current (Ord. 26-7) FAR, as in [Standard](bill6.md#envelope): Table 21-3.3 as amended for apartment and AMX districts and Table 21-3.4 for business districts |
| Open-space bonus | B-2 and BMX-3 up to 3.5, BMX-4 up to 7.5, as in Standard (see [Open-space bonus](../simulator-logic.md#open-space-bonus)) |
| Lot coverage | Current (Ord. 26-7) zoning, as in Standard: 60% in apartment and AMX districts, 100% in business districts |
| Height | The zoned height in the Citywide View (30 ft when the record has none), which the Max Height lever adds to or subtracts from; the Max Height slider in the Project View |
| Ground-floor retail | Allowed only in AMX-1/2/3 and BMX-3/4 |
| Unit cap | None |

## Height setbacks

The underlying zone's step-back rule, as in [Standard](bill6.md#height-setbacks).

**Not modeled:** HRS § 201H-38 lets a project be exempted from height setbacks, but the model applies the underlying zone's rule.

## Parking and loading

No 201H exemption is assumed, since HRS § 201H-38 exemptions are granted project by project, so the Article 6 requirements of [Standard](bill6.md#parking-and-loading) apply: 1 stall per 1,000 sq ft of dwelling area plus 1 per 500 sq ft of retail, 90% when combined, loading on lots over 7,500 sq ft, and bicycle parking. The location exemption of § 21-6.20(a) still applies parcel by parcel: 6,998 of 9,444 201H parcels need no stalls.

**Impact of the exemption.** Citywide View totals with all one-bedroom units and the Parking lever unset, retail off, with the Article 6 minimum on every parcel and then with the exemption applied: stalls fall from 623,825 to 120,384, units rise from 1,243,743 to 1,444,863, and parcels rated strong rise from 59 to 4,211. On the exempt parcels, dropping the stalls raises the average return on cost from 5.3% to 6.1%. The default 0.95 ratio puts housing stalls on exempt parcels too, so at the defaults, with retail off, the exemption changes nothing.

## Affordability

HHFDC requires no less than 50% plus one additional unit affordable to households at or below 140% AMI for both for-sale and rental projects. County guidance phrases it as a minimum of 50% + 1 of the sale or rental units or lots of the exempted project.

In the model the Affordability input sets the affordable share (default 51%); a value below 50% + 1 unit is rejected. Affordable units rent at the Affordable Unit Rent level (default 80% AMI) and market units at the Market Unit Rent level (default 120% AMI). There are no for-sale units.

***Note:*** the 51% affordable requirement was earlier split into 26% affordable rental units and 25% affordable for-sale units. Since for-sale units were removed (8/26/26), all 51% are rental.

## Fees, waivers and taxes

**Wastewater system facility charges:** waived for affordable rental dwelling units in accordance with HRS § 201H-36(a)(5).

**Building permit and plan review:** charged on market units only (the Impact Fees toggle's default, Market Units Only).

**Park dedication:** charged only on units not exempt under ROH 22-7.3(j)(3); 201H affordable rental units are exempt.

**BWS water charges:** waived on affordable units under the same toggle.

**GET exemption:** operating expenses are modeled at 19% of EGI, assuming the project is certified for the GET exemption (see [Operating expenses](../simulator-logic.md#operating-expenses)).

**Property tax:** full tax from year one; no exemption.

**Not modeled:** combining 201H with Bill 7. Some projects have used both pathways, and a project that qualifies for both could stack their grants and subsidies; each run is a single pathway.

## Default assumptions

| Input | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Unit mix | Half studio, half one-bedroom |
| Affordable share | 51% |
| Affordable / market rent | 80% / 120% AMI |
| Hard cost | $450 per sq ft |
| Operating expense ratio | 19% of EGI (GET exemption assumed) |
| Parking | 0.95 stalls per unit |
| Park dedication | In-lieu fee |
| Impact Fees toggle | Market Units Only |
| Permanent rate / minimum DSCR | 6.75% / 1.20 |
| Hold period | 8 years |
| Timeline | 18 months of permitting, 12 of construction and 9 of lease-up |
